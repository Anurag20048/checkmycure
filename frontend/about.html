About Us

Check MyCure is an AI-powered healthcare platform designed to bridge the gap between medical professionals and underserved populations.

Our mission is to make healthcare accessible to everyone, regardless of language, literacy level, or location.

Developed by a team of passionate students from Parul Institute of Computer Application, we combine cutting-edge technology with human-centered design.
